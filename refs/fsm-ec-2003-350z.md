# 2003 350Z factory service manual: EC section, extracted reference data

Source: Nissan FSM, section EC (Engine Control System), "2003 350Z", revision April 2004. Pages EC-1…EC-198 were supplied by the owner (parts 1–2). This file summarises specs and data tables for our tools; it is not a copy of the manual. **The PDFs themselves are not committed** (Nissan copyright, and large).

|---|---|---|

**Specification-value check (EC-133):** CONSULT-II's "DATA MONITOR (SPEC)" judges **B/FUEL SCHDL, A/F ALPHA-B1/B2 and MAS A/F SE-B1** against tolerances. Test conditions: >5,000 km driven, baro 98.3–104.3 kPa, ambient 20–30 °C, coolant 75–95 °C, warm transmission (M/T: drive 5 min), no electrical load, idle. These three are the "is the engine healthy" numbers for our HEALTH page.

## 4. Work support / active tests (CONSULT-II)
Work support: FUEL PRESSURE RELEASE · **IDLE AIR VOL LEARN** · **SELF-LEARNING CONT** (clear learned mixture trims) · EVAP SYSTEM CLOSE · (target idle/timing adjust).
Active tests: FUEL INJECTION · IGNITION TIMING · POWER BALANCE (per-cylinder cut) · ENG COOLANT TEMP · FUEL PUMP RELAY · PURG VOL CONT/V · VENT CONTROL/V · V/T ASSIGN ANGLE (command intake cam angle).

## 5. Learning procedures: **required after every flash / ECM disconnect** (EC-50…52)
1. **Accelerator Pedal Released Position Learning:** pedal released → key ON 2 s → OFF 10 s → ON 2 s → OFF 10 s.
2. **Throttle Valve Closed Position Learning:** pedal released → key ON → OFF and wait 10 s (listen for the throttle moving).
3. **Idle Air Volume Learning.** Preconditions: battery > 12.9 V at idle, coolant 70–100 °C, in neutral, no electrical loads, wheels straight, stationary, gearbox warm (M/T: drive 10 min).
   *Without CONSULT:* do steps 1 and 2 → warm up → key OFF 10 s → key ON, wait 3 s → **5× full press/release within 5 s** → wait 7 s → hold pedal down ~20 s until the MIL stops blinking and stays ON → release within 3 s → start, idle 20 s → rev 2–3× → check 650 ± 50 rpm and 15 ± 5°.
   *With a tool:* WORK SUPPORT → IDLE AIR VOL LEARN → START → wait for "CMPLT" (NisScan NDS II supports this).

**Bench ECU note:** an ECM swapped into the car requires **NATS initialisation and key registration** (BL-123 "ECM re-communicating function"). A bench ECU is fine for practice, but it won't start this car without that.

## 7. CAN bus (EC-30…38): broadcast data available at the OBD port
The **data link connector is a CAN node on every 2003 350Z variant.** Units on the bus: ECM, (TCM on A/T), combination meter + A/C amp, BCM, IPDM E/R, ABS or VDC/TCS/ABS unit, (low tyre pressure unit, steering angle sensor on VDC).

Signals the **ECM transmits** (M/T): engine speed · coolant temperature · accelerator pedal position · fuel consumption monitor · A/C compressor request/feedback · cooling fan speed request · MIL · ASCD lamps · (engine torque on TCS/VDC cars).
Signals from other units: **vehicle speed** (ABS/meter) · **fuel level** · stop lamp switch · door switches · lights/high beam · wipers · seat belt · ABS/brake/TCS warning lamps · tyre pressure.

➡️ The dash can **sniff these broadcasts passively** at the OBD port (CAN-H pin 6 / CAN-L pin 14) at full bus rate, with no K-line polling and no load on the ECM. We still have to identify the message IDs by sniffing.
